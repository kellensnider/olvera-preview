Paying for it over time

Financing

A new system is not a small purchase, and most people would rather not take the whole cost in one go. We work with Hearth, who handle the application and the lending decision, so you can see what a monthly payment might look like before you talk to anyone.

Financing

Check the rebates first

Most of what we install carries an Energy Trust of Oregon incentive, and we take it off your invoice rather than sending you away to claim it back. That is money you do not have to finance, so it is worth looking at the rebates before you work out a payment.

Rebates & offers

What it might cost a month

Put in a figure and the calculator gives you an estimated monthly payment. The numbers come from Hearth, not from us. They are an estimate rather than a quote for your job, and they are not an offer of credit.

Apply through Hearth

The application, the terms and the decision are Hearth’s rather than ours. We see whether it went through, so we can plan the work around it.
