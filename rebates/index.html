Rebates & Offers

Rebates & Offers

A large part of what a new system costs is covered by programmes most people never hear about. We are a trade ally of Energy Trust of Oregon, which means we file the paperwork and take the incentive off your invoice instead of sending you away to claim it back.

Current rebates and incentives

  • Up to $4,000

    Manufactured Home Discount Program

    Through Energy Trust of Oregon

    If you live in a manufactured home, Energy Trust of Oregon offers up to $4,000 toward a ducted heat pump and $3,500 toward a ductless system. Because we are an Energy Trust trade ally, the incentive is paid to us and comes straight off your invoice. You do not pay it up front and wait for a cheque.

    • Manufactured home in Energy Trust of Oregon service territory
    • Installed by an Energy Trust trade ally, which we are
    • Ducted heat pump up to $4,000, ductless up to $3,500

    Available through December 31, 2026

  • Up to $3,000

    Savings Within Reach

    Through Energy Trust of Oregon

    Energy Trust of Oregon pays more for the same upgrade when a household’s income falls inside a certain band. Same work, same equipment, a larger incentive. It is open to renters as well as owners. We can tell you whether your household qualifies before you commit to anything.

    • Household gross annual income between 80% and 150% of the county median
    • The exact limit depends on your county and how many people live in the home
    • Single-family home, manufactured home, or an attached residence up to four units
    • You sign an income verification form before the work starts
    • We file the application and the incentive comes off your invoice

    Ask us for current terms

    Needs your confirmation before launch. Confirm Olvera is enrolled in Energy Trust’s Savings Within Reach programme specifically. Their own information sheet says a trade ally has to be enrolled in Savings Within Reach to offer these amounts, and that is separate from ordinary trade ally status. If the enrolment is not in place this card comes down, and the Savings Within Reach column in the table below comes out with it.

  • No cost to qualifying households

    PCEF Home Energy Upgrades

    Through Portland Clean Energy Fund

    The Portland Clean Energy Fund pays for energy upgrades outright for income-qualified Portland homeowners. That covers heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, insulation and air sealing. We can tell you whether your household qualifies and handle the programme paperwork.

    • Portland homeowners under the programme income limit
    • Covers heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, insulation and air sealing

    Ask us for current terms

    Needs your confirmation before launch. Confirm Olvera is an approved PCEF contractor and how a homeowner starts the process with you, so we describe your actual role rather than the programme in general.

  • $250

    $250 Instant Rebate for Natural Gas Customers

    Through NW Natural

    NW Natural customers can take an instant rebate off qualifying work. We apply it at the invoice rather than sending you through a claim form.

    • Must be an active NW Natural residential gas customer
    • Applies to qualifying equipment, confirm with us before you book

    Ask us for current terms

    Needs your confirmation before launch. The relationship is confirmed, Olvera is in the NW Natural Preferred Contractors Alliance and the badge is now on the site. The amount is not. Eliseo gave $250; NW Natural publishes $200 for a furnace and $100 for a thermostat through that same programme. What the standard incentives actually pay

These are Energy Trust's published amounts as of January 1, 2026, taken from their own information sheet. What you get depends on the home and on the household, so all three columns are here rather than only the largest one.

How it works

You do not chase the paperwork, we do

  1. 1

    We look at the whole house

    Which programmes you qualify for depends on the house, the fuel you heat with, and in some cases your household income. We work that out before quoting.

    We quote with the incentive already taken off

    You see the real number you will pay, not a headline price with a rebate you have to go and claim afterwards.

  3. 3

    We file it

    Trade ally paperwork, programme checklists and the post-install documentation are ours to handle.
